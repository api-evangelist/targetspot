With peaks of 200 million monthly listeners according to Médiamétrie, the podcast audience in France is continuing to grow, particularly among the younger generations. This is a golden opportunity for advertisers to diversify their advertising channels. According to a recent Ipsos study, podcasts offer exceptional attention and highly effective targeting.
